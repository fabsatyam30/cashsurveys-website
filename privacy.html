Information Collected Automatically

We and our third-party service providers may use a variety of technologies, now and hereafter devised, that automatically record certain usage information about you ('Usage Information'). We use this information for a variety of purposes, including to help personalize our Service for you.

  • Log Information: Data about your use of the Service, such as IP address, browser type, internet service provider, referring/exit pages, operating system, date/time stamps, and related data, which is stored in log files.
  • Tracking Technologies: Including cookies, web beacons ('tracking pixels'), embedded scripts, location-identifying technologies, device fingerprinting, and in-app tracking methods.
  • Cookies: Small data files transferred to your computer to enable features, better understand interactions, and monitor online traffic routing.
  • Web Beacons (“Tracking Pixels”): Small graphic images embedded in web pages and email messages to count visitors and monitor navigation.
  • Embedded Scripts: Programming code temporarily downloaded onto your device to collect information about your interactions while connected.
  • Location-identifying Technologies: GPS, geo-filtering, and other location-aware technologies to locate you (sometimes precisely) for verifying location and delivering content.
  • Device Fingerprinting: Analyzing and combining sets of information elements from your device’s browser to create a “fingerprint” and uniquely identify your device.
  • In-App Tracking Methods: Mobile app tracking technologies that use device identifiers or “Ad IDs” to associate user activity across apps.
